## Phrases

- שָׁלוֹם = hello, goodbye, peace ; root: ש-ל-מ ; note: greeting and farewell both
- תּוֹדָה = thank you ; root: י-ד-ה
- בְּבַקָּשָׁה = please, you're welcome ; root: ב-ק-ש
- בֹּקֶר טוֹב = good morning
- לַיְלָה טוֹב = good night
- מָה שְׁלוֹמְךָ = how are you (to a man)
- מָה שְׁלוֹמֵךְ = how are you (to a woman) ; key: f

## Particles

- כֵּן = yes
- לֹא = no, not
- גַּם = also, too
- אֲבָל = but
- אוֹ = or

## Pronouns

- אֲנִי = I
- אַתָּה = you (m.sg)
- אַתְּ = you (f.sg)
- הוּא = he, it
- הִיא = she, it
- אֲנַחְנוּ = we
- הֵם = they (m)
- הֵן = they (f)
